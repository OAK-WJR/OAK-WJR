Hi, I'm Jiarui. I build tools that help people read across languages, and I build robots.

### Reading apps

- **ReadStair**: a free iPhone app for reading real English books. Scan a printed page or open an EPUB, and it underlines only the words you may not know; tap one to see what it means. In beta on TestFlight.\
  [readstair.com](https://readstair.com/) · [Open your book](https://readstair.com/open) · [Support](https://oak-wjr.github.io/readstair/)
- **LLBR**: take a photo of a page in a printed book, and it marks the words you probably don't know. On the App Store since October 2024.\
  [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/llbr/id6479208008) · [Code](https://github.com/OAK-WJR/LLBR) · [Support](https://oak-wjr.github.io/LLBR/)

### Robotics and hardware

- **MIT BWSI RACECAR, 2026**: in the online course, our team of three won the Micro Grand Prix finals. At the summer institute, our team placed 2nd in the final Grand Prix and won the Group Innovation Award, and I received the Dr. Bob Berman Disruptive Innovation Award.\
  Spring: [Code](https://github.com/BWSI-Best-Team/bwsi-micro-grand-prix-2026) · [Finals video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OlbtjRjY0dQ&t=950s) · Summer: [Simulator](https://github.com/BWSI-67/wallfacer) · [Race video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vsloWparWsk&t=16314s)
- **ArenaCapture**: a startup I co-founded with four friends. Its AutoScout estimates each FRC robot's scoring from match videos, so teams don't have to scout by hand.\
  [frc-autoscout.com](https://frc-autoscout.com/) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xj-HlX_2FPQ) · [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@frc-autoscout)
- **FRC Team 114**: programming, CAD and machining. The team won the Autonomous Award at the 2026 FIRST California Northern State Championship.\
  [Team page](https://www.thebluealliance.com/team/114) · [CNC notes](https://github.com/OAK-WJR/LAHS-CNC-team-notes)
- **EverFrame**: a 6-foot, five-axis metal robotic arm with custom harmonic-reducer joints, designed to be reused across FRC seasons.
- **MOOON**: a robot that drives on a vertical whiteboard and erases it, on custom PCBs (four versions).
- **Rocket flight computer**: one four-layer board for an amateur high-power rocket (STM32H743, IMU, barometer, GNSS, pyro and servo channels). A team project; I designed the board architecture. Not built yet.\
  [Code and CAD](https://github.com/OAK-WJR/rocket-flight-computer)
